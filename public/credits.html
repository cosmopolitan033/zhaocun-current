CURRENT is a personal creative development experiment by zhaocun.

Version 2

A procedural neon night drive with recycled architecture, light trails, three atmospheres, bloom, cruise/warp modes and a distraction-free experience. This version uses original procedural scene construction built with Three.js. No external 3D models or photographs are rendered.

Project source · Three.js — MIT
